FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the failure score?

It's a single number that shows how likely your print is to fail. A high score means the print is likely to fail. A low score means it's more likely to succeed. Zielda3D calculates it from temperature and design data using our proprietary regression model.

How does Zielda3D prevent failures?

Zielda3D checks your print before it starts. If the score is too high, it won't let you print yet. Instead, it shows corrective actions, such as a warning that your filament box is too humid. Once you've fixed the issues and the score is good, you're ready to print.

What do I need to get started?

Set up the Zielda3D hardware, connect it to your slicer software, and calibrate. That's all it takes before you see your first score.

How much does it cost?

Zielda3D starts at AED 20 per month and is designed to save users up to AED 100 per month in wasted material, energy and time.

Who is it for?

Anyone who prints: hobbyists, small businesses and print farms, especially where failed prints cause missed quotas and lost throughput.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Zielda3D is GDPR compliant.
